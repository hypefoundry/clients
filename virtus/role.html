About the role

A leading international law firm is seeking a Senior Legal Counsel to join its commercial disputes team in the City of London. This is a senior hire reporting directly to the Global Head of Dispute Resolution, with immediate responsibility for a portfolio of high-value commercial cases across EMEA.

The successful candidate will lead complex litigation and arbitration matters end-to-end, supervise mid-level associates, and contribute to the firm’s continued expansion into emerging markets. This role sits at partner-track level with a clear two-year path to equity.

Key responsibilities

  • Lead commercial litigation and international arbitration matters from instruction to resolution.
  • Advise FTSE-listed and private equity clients on cross-border disputes, regulatory investigations, and contentious M&A.
  • Supervise and mentor a team of four mid-level associates, shaping their casework and development.
  • Partner with the firm’s New York, Singapore, and Frankfurt offices on multi-jurisdictional matters.
  • Contribute to business development initiatives and originate new client relationships.
  • Represent the firm at industry conferences, speaker panels, and sector publications.

What you’ll bring

  • 8–12 years’ post-qualification experience at a Magic Circle, Silver Circle, or leading US firm in London.
  • Strong contentious background with first-chair experience on matters valued above £50m.
  • Qualified in England & Wales; additional US or EU bar qualifications a plus.
  • Demonstrable track record of originating work and building long-term client relationships.
  • Gravitas, pragmatism, and the confidence to operate at board and regulator level.

What’s on offer

  • Base salary of £100,000–£130,000 with a performance-linked bonus up to 40%.
  • Clear, documented two-year path to equity partnership.
  • Hybrid working with a minimum of three days in the Bank office.
  • Private healthcare, enhanced pension contributions, and 30 days’ leave.
  • Full subsidy for ongoing bar qualifications and executive education.

About the firm

Our client is a top-20 global law firm with 1,800 lawyers across 30 offices. Known for its disputes practice in particular, the firm was shortlisted as Disputes Firm of the Year at the most recent Legal Business Awards and has been consistently ranked Band 1 by Chambers for Commercial Litigation.

The firm is running a confidential search and has engaged Virtus exclusively. A full briefing pack, including firm name and financials, is available on application.

Two Directions

I’ve developed two distinct brand directions for The Guidance Co., designed to position The Guidance Co. as warm, modern, and parent-friendly without ever reading as clinical. Each concept explores a different creative direction: one boutique-hospitality, one modern product brand. Each gives you a genuine choice about the world your brand lives in, not just how it looks.

Concept 01 Concept 01

Concept 01

Inspired by boutique-wellness hospitality. Confident editorial restraint where the typography itself is the identity, paired with a refined G monogram for small-size applications. A warm mineral colour palette of bone, driftwood, and clay grounds the brand in the Australian coastal register without coastal cliché. Closer in feel to brands like Cultiver linen, Halcyon House, and a Kinfolk design feature than to a clinic or service brand.

Concept 01 Concept 01

Across stationery, web, and merch, this concept reads as boutique hospitality where typographic restraint and warm mineral neutrals carry the brand without needing a graphic mark.

Logo & Identity

An editorial italic-serif wordmark with an italicised “Co.” for warmth and signature, paired with a refined “G” monogram for small-size use. Embosses beautifully on bone card stock and embroidered into natural cotton totes.

Typography

GT Super Display or Canela Deck for editorial headlines, paired with Söhne humanist sans for body copy. Sentence case throughout for considered restraint. Hierarchy through scale and italics, never through capitalisation.

Website & Digital

Bone-and-clay palette across a hero focused on a single editorial photograph and a serif headline. Warm interior photography, cupped hands, a vase on a wooden table. Reads premium hospitality, not clinic.

Print & Merch

Embossed bone-stock business cards, linen-bound notebooks, embroidered “G.” on natural cotton totes. Foil-stamped clay accents on letterpress card. The materials carry the brand as much as the design does.

Concept 02 Concept 02

Concept 02

Modern, approachable, digital-native. A lowercase wordmark anchored by a pecan-coloured period that becomes the brand’s defining primitive and scales across logo, pattern, web, and illustration. The dot can read as a pause before a reaction, a meeting point, or a grounded centre. The oatmilk, sage, and pecan colour palette feels warm and modern. Closer in feel to brands like Kin Fertility and The Indigo Project than to a healthcare provider.

Across favicons, patterns, web, and social, this concept is built around a single pecan dot that scales as logo, pattern, illustration, and watermark. One primitive carries the entire brand system.

Logo & Identity

Lowercase humanist sans wordmark in GT America with a pecan-coloured period as the brand’s defining atom. The single pecan dot scales as favicon, app icon, embossing, and architectural element on the website hero.

Typography

Single-family system in GT America. Hierarchy through weight, width, and case rather than family. Lowercase headlines for voice, sentence-case body copy for readability. One typeface, full system depth.

Website & Digital

Oatmilk, sage, pecan, and basalt palette. Overlapping-circle hero composition where the pecan dot scales to architectural size beside a circular family photograph. Lowercase quote tiles with a single pecan accent word.

Pattern & Illustration

Scattered pecan dots applied to notebook endpapers and tote linings as the brand’s primary pattern. Stippled dotwork illustrations of cups, plants, and hands for office art and Zoom backgrounds.

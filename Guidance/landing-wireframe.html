the small, quiet work of raising humans.

A Sunshine Coast practice supporting NDIS-funded and private families. Real-life strategies for real-life moments. Warm, evidence-informed, parent-friendly.

We work with families when behaviour, big feelings, or daily rhythms are making everyday life harder than it needs to be — and we walk alongside parents long enough for the strategies to actually land.

what we do

Three areas of practice. One considered approach.

Each service stands alone, but most families benefit from a combination — depending on what's hardest right now. A first conversation helps work out the right starting point.

service photo

01 · Service

Behaviour Support

Practical, evidence-informed support for children whose behaviour is making everyday life harder than it needs to be — at home, at school, or out in the community. We start by understanding the behaviour, not labelling it.

  • Functional behaviour assessments
  • Positive behaviour support plans
  • In-home strategy sessions
  • Coaching for parents and carers
  • School and early-learning collaboration

02 · Service

Emotional Regulation

Supporting children to understand, name, and move through big feelings — without shutting them down or hurrying them up. Strategies that work in real life, not just in therapy rooms.

  • Co-regulation strategies for parents
  • Sensory and somatic tools
  • Anxiety and big-feeling support
  • Building emotional vocabulary
  • Regulation that holds up at school and home
learn more about emotional regulation →
service photo

03 · Service

Parent Guidance

One-on-one parent coaching when the strategies you've tried aren't landing — or when you'd rather have someone in your corner before things get harder. Reflective, practical, no judgement.

  • Personalised strategy sessions
  • Sleep, mealtimes, transitions, screen-time
  • Sibling dynamics
  • Reflective parent coaching
  • Support that meets your family where it is

how it works

Three steps from first hello to ongoing support.

01

Get in touch

A short enquiry form, a phone call, or an email. Tell us what's hardest right now and we'll come back within one business day.

02

First conversation

A free, no-commitment 20-minute call to talk through your family's situation, what kind of support might help, and whether we're the right fit.

03

Ongoing support

If we're a fit, we'll plan an approach — in-home sessions, parent coaching, or a combination — and walk alongside you for as long as it's helpful.

questions

Common questions, answered.

If your question isn't here, the first conversation is the right place for it. Always free, never a sales call.

  • Are you NDIS registered?
  • How does pricing work for private clients?
  • Do you work in-home, in-clinic, or via telehealth?
  • What ages do you work with?
  • How quickly can we get started?
  • Do you collaborate with schools and other practitioners?

Ready when you are.

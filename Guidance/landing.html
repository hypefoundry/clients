Why work with The Guidance Co?

Complex care expertise

More than a decade supporting children, young people, and adults across Child Safety, Youth Justice, residential care, schools, and community settings.

In-placement delivery

We work where behaviours occur in placements, schools, and community settings, providing practical support alongside the people working with the individual every day.

Trauma-informed practice

Our approach is informed by an understanding of trauma, attachment, and lived experience, helping create strategies that are realistic, consistent, and sustainable.

Practical, not just paperwork

Our focus is on strategies that can be used in everyday situations. The goal is to provide support that leads to meaningful change, not reports that sit on a shelf.

Frequently asked questions

  • Are you NDIS registered and aligned to the PBS Capability Framework?+
    Yes. We're an NDIS-registered behaviour support provider operating under the PBS Capability Framework, with current Working with Children Check and Blue Card credentials.
  • Do you accept Child Safety and Youth Justice brokerage?+
    Yes. We invoice CSO and YJ brokerage directly, alongside NDIS plan-managed and self-managed funding. We can also work on direct-billed engagements outside the NDIS where required.
  • How do you work with residential care and kinship placements?+
    In the placement. We coach the team carrying the young person — residential workers, kinship and foster carers — and stay long enough for the regulation and behaviour strategies to land in practice, not just on paper.
  • What ages and order types do you work with?+
    Children and young people 4–18 on Child Protection Orders, Youth Justice orders, and at the edge of statutory involvement. We work across short-term, long-term, kinship, foster, and residential placement types.
  • What's your turnaround on assessments and reports?+
    Most functional behaviour assessments turn around inside 4–6 weeks from referral. Court and review reports are scoped to deadline at the scoping conversation — we don't take work we can't deliver on time.
  • Do you provide reports for court, case plans, and reviews?+
    Yes. We provide functional behaviour reports, PBS plans, restrictive-practice reviews, and supporting documentation for case planning, ongoing reviews, and Children's Court matters where appropriate.
  • Do you offer secondary consultation between formal sessions?+
    Yes — and it's often the most useful part of an engagement. Teams can call between sessions for a 15-minute consult on what's coming up. It's built into the engagement scope, not billed separately.

The Template Library.

A complete Instagram, LinkedIn and Facebook content system built for The Guidance Co. The Figma library contains 80+ ready-to-edit frames grouped into nine recurring post types, each rendered in three native formats (Stories, feed portrait, square), with the square format also working as a LinkedIn post.

The aim is simple: give the practice a steady, professional online presence, somewhere referrers, carers and educators can land and quickly get a feel for who you are. It's also built to be sustainable, so it can keep running alongside the actual work without becoming another full-time job.

Each of the nine post types is labelled A through I, and each one comes in three sizes (Stories, feed portrait, and square), so the same idea can be used across all three formats. To open a template, just search its name in Figma (for example, "Story v7.0").

Making a post.

Once you've got the rhythm, each post takes about fifteen minutes from start to scheduled. Here's how it goes.

01.Pick the template

Think about what you want to say, then pick the matching post type from the library above (A through I). In Figma, type the frame name into the layers panel search (for example, "Square v7.0") and it'll jump you straight there. Right-click and duplicate so you're working on a fresh copy.

02.Write the copy

Add your words to the template. If you're not sure where to start, the caption frameworks in the content direction guide are a useful jumping-off point: one idea to share, one example to illustrate it, one invitation at the end. The design will hold whatever you write, as long as it fits the field.

03.Add an image (if the template needs one)

Templates G, H, and I are the photo-led ones. Drop your image into the frame, making sure it fits the brand: real photos of your practice space, hands at work, materials on the desk, or the Sunshine Coast itself. Never identifiable young people, ever.

04.Export the file

Right-click the frame and export as a PNG at 2×. That gives you a crisp file that won't go fuzzy on Instagram. Save it with a filename like 2026-06-15-tip-co-regulation.png so it's easy to find later.

05.Schedule it ahead

Schedule the post in Meta Business Suite (the free tool from Instagram and Facebook for managing posts). Try to plan around two weeks ahead so the rhythm keeps going even on busy weeks. The strongest feed posts are worth cross-posting to Facebook at the same time.

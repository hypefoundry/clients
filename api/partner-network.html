01 · HERO
B2B inbound page. Two audiences: professional firms wanting to become an API partner, and not-yet-partnered firms with a one-off client to refer. Page stays simple, hero leads with what API's partner network looks like.
Eyebrow

A short tag positioning this as the partner-network page.

H1 · Headline

We work with brokers, planners, accountants, conveyancers, and quantity surveyors.

Sub / Value prop

One supporting line. Names what the partner network looks like and the two reasons a firm lands here: to join, or to refer a one-off client.

Existing partner firms

Logo carousel · greyscale logos of API's existing partner firms (broker, planner, accountant, QS, conveyancer). Auto-scrolling or paginated. Placeholder count: 10+ logos.
02 · LOGO CAROUSEL
Visual proof of the existing network without listing every firm with a description. Greyscale logos so they don't compete with brand colours. Auto-scroll or paginate as the list grows.
03 · THE FORM
Dual-purpose form. Visitor picks one of two intents: "I'd like to join the partner network" or "I'd like to refer a client (we're not partnered yet)". The form fields stay the same; the intent radio routes the submission to the right HubSpot pipeline.

Frames the form as the way to start the conversation.

Form · partner inquiry
Field 1 · Intent (radio)

Two options: "I'd like to join the partner network" or "I'd like to refer a client (we're not partnered yet)".

Field 2 · Your firm

Firm name + your role (broker, planner, accountant, etc).

Field 3 · Your name + email

Contact details for follow-up.

Field 4 · Client's name + email (conditional)

Shown only when intent is "refer a client". Optional context line for the situation.

Field 5 · Note (optional)

Free-text. What they want API to know.

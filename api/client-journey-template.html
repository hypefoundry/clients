01 · HERO
Video is the asset on this page. The client tells their own story in their own words. Everything else around it is structural scaffolding: who, what archetype, how long, what changed.
Schema: VideoObject (the testimonial), Person (the client), BreadcrumbList (Home › Client Journeys › [slug]). All three live on this hero block.
Breadcrumb

Home · Client Journeys · [client name or slug]

Eyebrow

A short tag naming the archetype (e.g. Rentvesting, Family, First-time, Portfolio scaling, Women-led).

H1 · Outcome line

Names who the client is and what changed for them. Built for the search "[archetype] property investor success story".

Sub · 1 line

A single line of context. Names where they were when they came to API and where they are now.

Client video · short testimonial (typically 1-3 minutes)

02 · QUICK FACTS
A four-tile strip a visitor can read in under three seconds. Pre-empts the "is this person like me" question before the visitor commits to scrolling. Verifiable facts only, no claims.
03 · BEFORE
A short context block. One or two sentences naming what brought the client to API. Names the problem in the visitor's language so they can self-locate.

Names where the client was before API.

Body · 1-2 sentences

A short paragraph in plain language. Names the situation, the pain, and what they had tried already. Stays in the client's voice, not in marketing voice.

Frames the three stages of this client's engagement.

Stage 1 · Discover

Names this client's discover stage.

A single line of what happened in this phase for this specific client. Names the gap the assessment surfaced or the goal they set.

Stage 2 · Research

Names the research stage.

A single line of what we looked at and why. Names the market, the criteria, and the trade-off the client made.

Stage 3 · Partnership

Names the partnership stage.

A single line on the long-arc support. Names whether they've returned for a second or third property.

04 · THE JOURNEY
Mirrors the methodology on the Process pages (Discover · Research · Partnership) so the visitor sees the abstract method made concrete on this one client. One line per stage keeps it scannable.
05 · OUTCOME
What changed for the client. Narrative outcomes only, no specific dollar returns or growth percentages (ACL §18). Two or three bullet outcomes, in plain language. The video carries the emotion; this block carries the structure.

Names the outcome for this client.

Body · short paragraph

A short paragraph naming what changed. Plain language, narrative, not a sales line. Stays compliant: describes outcomes, not predicted returns.

  • Outcome bullet 1. Plain, specific, verifiable.
  • Outcome bullet 2. Plain, specific, verifiable.
  • Outcome bullet 3 (optional). Plain, specific, verifiable.
Compliance footnote

A short footnote acknowledging individual results vary. No specific yield, growth, or return claims (per ACL §18).

Editorial pull-quote · Review schema

A short, memorable line from the client's video, treated as large editorial type. One sentence at most.

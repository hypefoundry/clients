Are you an Active or Aspiring investor?

Sub / Value prop

The supporting line. Names the four factors by name (Finance, Focus, Support, Flexibility), the five-minute timing, and the same-day outcome the visitor gets back.

Take the 5-minute assessment

Explains why the assessment exists.

Origin story · Emma + Chris

Two paragraphs telling the story of how Emma and her consultant Chris built the four-factor scale in December 2025. Frames it as an internal sales tool that became too useful not to share.

Body · the dual commitment

A short paragraph naming the two active paths API takes with every visitor: we work with the ready, we educate the aspiring. The assessment routes each visitor to the right path. Both are active engagements, not a refusal frame.

02 · WHY THIS EXISTS
Sets up the methodology before explaining the mechanics. Origin story humanises the framework so it doesn't feel like a corporate scoring rubric.
03 · THE FOUR FACTORS
The methodological core of the page. Each factor gets a full block: what it is, what high looks like, what low looks like.
FAQPage schema: each factor turns into 4-6 Q&A pairs (16-24 extractable facts total). Direct LLM-citation surface for "what is the four-factor readiness scale", "am I ready to invest in property", "what does a buyer's agent check".

Factor 2 · Focus

Names the Focus factor.

A few sentences explaining Focus: whether the visitor zooms in on the details (door colour, fittings, exact spec) or on the results (the investment outcome and capital growth). High Focus means results-focused. Low Focus means detail-perfectionist, which slows the process and rarely closes.

Factor 3 · Support

Names the Support factor.

A few sentences explaining Support: how the visitor wants to share the property research, selection, and execution work with a specialist team. High Support means handing the heavy lifting over to API end to end. Lower Support means keeping more of the work in the visitor's own hands and bringing API in at key moments.

Article card variants

Card options for the founder page's recent press features. Same content, three different framings, all on the design-system type and colour styles. Pick one direction and we standardise on it.

  • A · Bordered grid
  • B · Editorial minimal
  • C · Elevated card

Variant A

Bordered grid

Cells divided by hairline grid lines. Image, a larger headline, then a footer row with category, date, and a small square link arrow. Structured and newsroom-like.

Editorial minimal

Borderless and airy, magazine-style. Tall image, a tiny uppercase category, a compact title, and a short standfirst. Lets the images carry the section.

Elevated card

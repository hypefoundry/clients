Twelve years refining
The API Approach

Emma Allen has spent more than twelve years, and nearly two decades in property, refining the approach behind every brief. It is built on disciplined research, tested through changing markets, and guided by one principle: strategy comes before property.

An award-winning entrepreneur, she is a contributing author in two best-selling Australian business anthologies and co-host of the Property Boss Mums podcast.

Today the specialist team carries that approach forward, holding every brief to the same standard: only the properties that survive the research reach a client.

Our research culling process

This is the discipline behind that selection. Refined over the years, it assesses each location against the fundamentals that drive growth and tenant appeal: economic, population and infrastructure trends, town planning, public and private investment, and the risks sitting beneath the surface. FAQs

Common questions from investors

  • How long does an engagement usually last?
    It depends on where an investor is starting from. When someone is financially and emotionally ready, we can move from strategy to selection in one to two weeks, with a contract secured within a matter of days. If there are steps to prepare first, we hold off on shortlisting until everything is in order. From there the timeline follows the property: something recently completed, or a build. Either way we support the investor over the month or months until they have their keys, and we help transition the property across to their property manager. Many clients maintain a long term relationship, touching base to test the market again, revisit how values have grown or cashflow has changed, and regrouping for their second, third or fourth property.
  • How does API get paid?
    Our service is complimentary. As licensed real estate agents, we receive a marketing fee from the vendor when a property is purchased. Unlike a traditional real estate model, we don’t present every opportunity we’re offered. Each one passes through our Research Culling Process before it’s matched to a client’s strategy and brief.
  • Are you a buyer’s agent?
    No. We are researchers first and foremost, advocates for research and the fundamentals of sound investing. We are not licensed to act for you in the transaction, and we work as a property consultancy rather than a transactional buyer’s agent. How a property performs is only one part of the picture: the ability to invest well is shaped by lending, buying structures, economics and legislation, and the many moving parts an investor has to navigate in a market that keeps changing. Clients come to us to facilitate the whole of it, the strategy, the shortlisting and the buying service, with support that continues property after property.
  • What kinds of properties do you focus on?
    New property, primarily: house and land packages, townhouses and apartments, dual-occupancy and duplexes, and property held inside a self-managed super fund. You are the first owner, which means full depreciation, current build standards, fewer surprises at settlement, and a cleaner picture for the lender. We are also expanding into commercial property to support SMSF investors, as the limited recourse borrowing arrangement (LRBA) rules for residential property held inside super have changed. The reasons sit in full on the Research and Selection page.
  • Does API build the property or manage construction?
    No. API researches, supports and facilitates the buying journey. The builder remains responsible for the build itself, including construction quality and any defect resolution. Our role is to support alignment between the outcome and the original strategy, from the brief through to settlement.
  • Do you publish a list of available properties?
    No. Properties are matched to a brief, not browsed off a shelf. The selection page explains why a public list works against the methodology and what the alternative looks like.
  • Can you work with my existing broker, accountant or financial planner?
    Yes. Most clients already have trusted professionals. We work alongside them, sharing the information they need to support your investment strategy and keeping everyone aligned throughout the process.
  • What happens after I take the Investor Quiz?
    You get a short summary of where you sit, the move that would put you in a stronger position, and a clear next step. Either a strategy session, the Active Investor Path, or a tailored path through the resources. The Investor Quiz takes about five minutes.

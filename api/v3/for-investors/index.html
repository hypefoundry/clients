2027 · Tax reform context

Tax reform is reshaping the property investment landscape.

From July 2027, the 50 per cent capital gains tax discount is being replaced and negative gearing is largely being limited to new-build properties. Existing investors are largely protected by grandfathering. Twelve years ago we chose new property as a specialism. Today, that choice does more of the work.

Built for the Active Investor.

You have capital, clarity, and not enough weekends. This is the page for clients who are ready to move and want a small team handling the work alongside them.

  • Strategy before selection.

    Every engagement starts with the plan, not the property. The brief is built around your goals, your capital, and the life the portfolio is meant to fund.

  • Fit over volume.

    A small caseload by choice. Most properties reviewed never make it to a brief. The work of saying no is what protects every yes.

  • Partnership, not transaction.

    Most clients buy more than one property with us. The conversation continues for years, across portfolio decisions, not just a single settlement.

The engagement

Three stages. One continuous engagement.

  1. 1

    Strategy. The plan before the property.

    Goals, structure, timeline, capital allocation. We work out where you are, where you want to be, and what the portfolio needs to look like to get you there.

  2. 2

    Research and selection.

    Market screen, suburb research, due diligence, client-fit match. The work of finding a property that fits the brief, then checking it against the brief once more before it is presented.

  3. 3

    Partnership. The next property is the same conversation.

    Settlement, then the long arc. Portfolio reviews, re-strategy when life changes, and the next property when it is time.

The fee model

Our service is complimentary.

As licensed real estate agents working on the buyer side, we receive a marketing fee from the developer when a property is purchased. It is the same model that pays your mortgage broker.

The discipline that holds the model honest is the rejection rate. Most properties we review never reach a client brief. The strategy is written before any property is considered, and the property has to match the strategy before it is presented to you.

"Our reputation is worth far more than a fast fee."

Emma Allen, Founder

Common questions before the assessment.

  • How long does an engagement usually last?
    Most clients move through strategy, selection, and settlement over a number of months. The partnership stage continues from there. Many clients are still on the books years later, working through their second, third, or fourth property.
  • How does API get paid?
    The service is complimentary to you. As licensed real estate agents working on the buyer side, we receive a marketing fee from the developer when a property is purchased, the same model that pays your mortgage broker. Most properties we review never reach a client brief. Our reputation is worth far more than a fast fee.
  • What kinds of properties do you focus on?
    New property, primarily. The reasons sit on the Research and Selection page: depreciation, build quality, fewer surprises at settlement, and a cleaner picture for the lender. Strategy drives the call, not personal preference.
  • Do you publish a list of available properties?
    No. Properties are matched to a brief, not browsed off a shelf. The selection page explains why a public list works against the methodology and what the alternative looks like.
  • Can you work with my existing broker, planner, or accountant?
    Yes. Most clients arrive with at least one professional already in place. We work alongside that network, or refer to ours where there is a gap.
  • What happens after I take the assessment?
    You get a short summary of where you sit, the move that would put you in a stronger position, and a clear next step. Either a strategy session, the Active Investor Path, or a tailored path through the resources. The assessment takes about five minutes.

The highlight

One or two words, set in PP Editorial New italic and tiffany, to lift a line. The italic serif is constant; the colour defaults to tiffany but can vary.

A property consultancy that brokers actually recommend

The recent reforms changed the conversation, not what makes a good investment

  • Font. PP Editorial New, italic, always. Never the sans in italic.
  • Colour. Tiffany 500 by default; a page may deepen or lighten it to suit the surface.
  • Restraint. One highlight per line, and rarely more than one per section.

On tiffany backgrounds

On a light tiffany band, headings and body take deep tiffany ink. On a dark tiffany band, type reverses to light tiffany. The highlight stays tiffany 500 throughout.

Voice

Tone of voice

Emma's measured Australian voice. Research-led and persuasive, evidence over emotion, never soft or aggressive.

Updated 31 July 2026 · 2 min read

Research-led. Reach for the vocabulary of the work: research, assess, scrutinise, cull. Evidence, not adjectives.

Persuasive, not soft. Data-forward and confident. "Generational wealth" is approved, it is Emma's own language.

Australian, measured. Australian spelling. Avoid Americanisms and aggressive verbs like "screens hard".

Earned lines. One memorable, authentic line per section. Don't end everything on a symmetrical slogan.

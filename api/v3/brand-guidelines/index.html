Brand overview

How Active Property Investing looks and sounds. A specialist, research-led property consultancy with a calm and elegant visual language: teal carries the palette, hero imagery ranges from atmospheric landscapes to people in aspirational, positive settings, an editorial serif highlights key words, and the copy is clear, confident and grounded in research.

Look

The palette is grounded in ink and white, with teal as the accent. PP Editorial New italic draws attention to key words. Hero imagery is atmospheric and expansive: softly lit landscapes, city views and people in aspirational, positive settings, layered with a translucent teal wash. Low contrast, hazy horizons and generous negative space give the headline room to lead.

Voice

Research-led and persuasive, with evidence over emotion. Clear, direct and confident, with warmth and authority in equal measure.

Feel

Generous white space, clean, quietly confident.

Typography

SF Pro Display is the primary typeface, used in three weights across headlines, subheadlines and body copy. Keywords and short phrases are emphasised in PP Editorial New Italic.

SF Pro Display · primary typeface

Proven through every cycle

SF Pro Display is the primary typeface across the system. Its three weights create hierarchy across headlines, subheadlines and body copy, while PP Editorial New Italic is reserved for keywords and short highlighted phrases.

The eyebrow tag

Small, uppercase and letter-spaced, the eyebrow labels a section above the headline.

The highlight

Keywords and short phrases are set in PP Editorial New Italic and teal to lift a line. The italic serif is constant; the colour defaults to teal but can vary.

A property consultancy that brokers actually recommend

  • Font - PP Editorial New, italic, always. Never the sans in italic.
  • Colour - Teal is the default highlight colour. Lighter and darker shades maintain contrast across different backgrounds.
  • Restraint - One highlight per line, and rarely more than one per section.

On teal backgrounds

On a light teal band, headings and body copy use deep teal ink. On a dark teal band, type reverses to light teal. On a solid teal 500 band, all type reverses to white, with emphasis carried by the italic alone. The highlight remains teal 500 on light and dark bands.

Voice

Tone of voice

Clear, confident and grounded in research. Persuasive without hype, with evidence doing the work.

The test. If a line could not be said by Emma to a friend's sister at dinner, it is not on brand.

Research-led

Reach for the vocabulary of the work: research, assess, scrutinise, cull. Evidence over adjectives.

Specific over generic

Numbers beat adjectives and names beat roles. "Twelve years" says more than "experienced".

Calm and confident

Data-forward and quietly assured. "Wealth through property" is Emma's own language. No urgency, no hype.

Sell the approach

Describe how the work is done and let the outcome be implied. One earned line per section.

Wherever you're starting from

Property investing, for the long term

Whether you are investing for the first time, growing what you have started, or rebuilding after a change, the hard part is rarely ambition. It is having the clarity and confidence to take the next step.

  • Define a clear strategy

    Which market, which structure, whether now is the time. At any stage, the right call is rarely obvious.

  • Remove the legwork

    Researching markets, making sense of data, finding the information to make well informed decisions.

  • Build the right team around you

    Brokers, planners, accountants, and people on the ground where you're buying. Pulling that together takes time and the connections to know who to trust.

“In 2014 I founded Active Property Investing to do things differently.”

Strategy first, disciplined research, and partnerships that last. Twelve years on, that approach draws many women balancing a career, a business, and a family, who want to build wealth through property without giving up the life they are already building.

Meet the founder

What working with us looks like

  • Strategy before property

    Your goals set the brief, then disciplined research decides what fits. Every recommendation is held to the standard we would apply to our own portfolio.

  • A small, specialist team

    Research, analysis and due diligence are carried by the team.

  • A long-term partnership

    Many clients return for their second, third and fourth property, with support that continues well beyond settlement.

  • Clear communication

    Efficient, responsive communication through every stage of your journey, so you always know where things stand.

  • A trusted network

    Brokers, planners, accountants, and conveyancers who already work alongside the team.

Our philosophy

Property is the vehicle, strategy is the engine

The outcome is rarely decided on just picking a property. It begins earlier, with the strategy that guides the decision and the research behind it. Investing fits around your career, your family, and your finances, while the process keeps moving forward.

How we think about fit
The three-step consulting approach

Our approach to property investing

  • Strategy

    Strategy comes before any property. Together we define your goals and set the investment brief that guides every recommendation, a discipline refined over twelve years.

  • Research

    Our Disciplined Research Culling Process weighs each market against the fundamentals that drive long-term demand and tenant appeal, narrowing hundreds of possibilities to the few that fit your brief.

  • Support

    A specialist team stays with you at every stage. Our relationship rarely ends at settlement. Many clients return for their second, third and fourth property.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How involved do I need to be?
    As much or as little as you like. The team spends the hours screening the market and presents the findings, so you have what you need to decide with confidence, then carries it through to settlement. Updates come in writing, so you stay across it all without chasing anyone by phone.
  • What does it cost to work with API?
    Our service is complimentary. As licensed real estate agents, we receive a marketing fee from the vendor when a property is purchased. Every opportunity still passes through our Research Culling Process before it is matched to your brief, so what reaches you has already earned its place.
  • How do I know if I am ready to invest?
    That is exactly what the Investor Quiz is for. Five minutes gives you a clear read on where you stand and the first step worth taking, whether you are ready now or building toward it.
  • What if I already own an investment property?
    You are in good company. Plenty of our clients come to us to grow a portfolio they have already started. We reassess the strategy against where you are now and where the market is, and plan the next move from there.

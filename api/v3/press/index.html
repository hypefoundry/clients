Published work

Chapters by Emma Allen

Contributing author to two Amazon best-selling Australian business anthologies, with a new book due in late 2026. Royalties from her chapters support scholarships and charitable initiatives.

  • Back Yourself

    An AusMumpreneur anthology from Peace Mitchell and Katy Garner, written in 2020 through the uncertain months after Australia's first nationwide lockdown. Emma writes about carving your own pathway and creating your own opportunities as an entrepreneur and businesswoman.

  • The Women Changing the World

    An anthology of authors from around the world. Emma's chapter follows her philanthropic journey with Global Village Housing and Houses for Cambodia, from her first visit to Siem Reap and the family who received API's first gifted home to the school projects that grew from it.

  • A new book on authentic leadership

    Coming late 2026, a new book featuring Emma's writing on authentic leadership, continuing what three decades in business and property have taught her. More to share closer to launch.

The foundation

Every strategy begins with four factors

  • Finance

    Capital, borrowing capacity and the financial structure supporting the strategy.

  • Focus

    The investment principles that guide decisions, rather than reacting to headlines or short-term noise.

  • Support

    How much of the process you want the team to manage, and how involved you want to be along the way.

  • Flexibility

    How broad the brief is across markets, locations and property types, giving the research room to work.

The engagement

A three-step consulting approach

Every engagement follows the same three stages. Strategy establishes the brief. Research identifies opportunities. Support carries the work through settlement and beyond.

Step 1 · Strategy

Strategy is the engine

Before any property is considered, we define the strategy that guides every recommendation. Goals, structure and capital form the inputs. A clear investment brief becomes the output.

Together we explore your goals, timeframe, ownership structure and financial position before considering the four factors that shape the investment brief: Finance, Focus, Support and Flexibility.

The four factors behind every strategy
Step 2 · Research

Research is the discipline

Our Research and Culling Process assesses market fundamentals, growth drivers, supply and demand, tenant appeal, infrastructure investment and future resale competitiveness. Every opportunity is tested against your investment brief, current market conditions and the fundamentals that drive long-term wealth creation.

The strongest investment is not always the one first imagined.

In FY2026 we knocked back 84% of the properties we assessed. Only about 16% ever reach a client, so by the time an opportunity reaches you, it has already survived that scrutiny.

See how a property earns its place
Step 3 · Support

New property is a process

How long this stage runs depends on the property. A recently completed property can move quickly. A property that's still under development takes longer, with support continuing throughout.

Contracts, pre-settlement preparation, settlement and handover all need coordinating. Our role is to keep everything moving, keep communication clear, and bring together the right professionals at each stage, so you always know what's happening and what's next.

API is not the builder. The builder remains responsible for construction quality and defect resolution. Independent inspections and legal review remain the buyer's responsibility.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

  • What does API specialise in?
    New property. House and land packages, duplexes and dual occupancies, townhouses, terraces, boutique apartments and near-completed homes, across ten Australian markets. API has specialised in new property since 2014.
  • How does API decide which properties to recommend?
    Through continuous research across ten markets and a three-level screen: builder and developer, location and leading indicators, build and inclusions. There is no public list. Every recommendation is matched to your strategy.
  • What happens after I take the Investor Quiz?
    You receive a short summary of what you shared, the one move that would put you in a stronger position, and a clear set of options. No score, no band, no label.
  • Does API build property or oversee construction?
    No. API researches, supports and facilitates the buying journey. The builder remains responsible for the build itself, including construction quality and defect resolution. Independent inspections and legal review sit with the buyer.
  • How long does a typical engagement run?
    Often one to three years from the first conversation. Strategy refreshes happen as life and the market move, and many clients return for the next property when the timing is right.
  • What if I am not ready to act yet?
    The Active Investor Path keeps you close to the strategy while the picture clears. API Property Letters every quarter, and an open door whenever the situation moves.

The kit

A ready-to-run package for approaching new referral partners on social, in the same voice as the partner page and the email sequence.

Updated 31 July 2026 · 2 min read

Scope. This kit is the strategy, templates and assets. Execution, the sending, list-building and ongoing content, sits with API and is outside the campaign scope.

What is different about cold outreach

The partner page and emails speak to partners who already know API. This kit speaks to people who do not yet. Same tone and positioning, but each message has to earn attention fast and lead with relevance. The one thing every touch protects is the prospect's real fear, that aligning with a property firm could cost them their client relationship. The answer, every time: your client stays yours.

Targeting

Who we target

Prioritise prospects who already sit next to a property-buying client. Each type refers for a different reason, so each gets its own lead angle.

Updated 31 July 2026 · 2 min read

Mortgage broker

Clients are actively financing property, and the broker wants them to buy well and stay theirs. Lead with the promise that their client stays yours.

Financial planner

Property sits inside a wealth or SMSF strategy, so they need disciplined, research-led support. Lead with strategy and methodology.

Accountant

Clients are asking about tax-efficient property in the wake of the reforms. Lead with the reform moment.

Quantity surveyor

Already works with new-property investors on depreciation, a natural earlier-stage fit. Lead with the shared new-property focus.

Cadence per prospect: Connect, then on accept a first message, a follow-up around day four, a value touch around day ten, then leave the door open. Never more than these touches. No chasing.

Proof

What we lead with

The proof behind every message. Reach for these; do not invent new claims.

Updated 31 July 2026 · 2 min read

Specialist. New property only, twelve years, Australia-wide across 10 cities and regions.

Selectivity. We decline 84% of the properties we assess. Only 16% reach a client (FY2026).

Reputation-safe. Structured introductions and clear updates, strategy to settlement. Your client stays yours.

The moment. The recent reforms have changed the conversation, not what makes a good property investment.

The hook. Exclusive Partner Speaking Sessions, webinar or in-person, limited dates for 2026, with the partner's name on the invitation.

Named humans. Emma Allen (Founder) and Chris Newell (Principal Consultant) run the partner conversations.

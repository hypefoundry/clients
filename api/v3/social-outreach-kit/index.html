The kit

Strategy, templates and finished assets for opening conversations with new referral partners on social.

How to use the kit

Start with the referral partner profiles to choose the right angle, then use the proof points to keep each message specific and consistent. Select the connection request, first message or follow-up that matches the stage of the conversation. The visuals section brings together the finished social graphics and partner one-pager, available individually or as a single download.

Targeting

Referral partners

Focus on professionals already supporting clients who are considering property. Effective outreach recognises each partner's role and shows how API complements the work they already do.

Mortgage broker

Their clients are already financing property and need confidence in what they buy. API adds disciplined research and purchase support while the broker's client relationship remains firmly theirs.

Financial planner

Property often sits within a broader wealth or SMSF strategy. API complements the planner's work with a strategy-first process, disciplined research and clear communication through to settlement.

Accountant

The negative gearing changes are prompting clients to ask how established and new property compare. API brings more than 12 years of new-property specialism and clear client education to that conversation.

Quantity surveyor

They already support new-property investors through depreciation. That shared focus creates a natural point of collaboration, from strategy and property selection through to settlement.

Suggested cadence: Send a connection request. Once accepted, follow with the first message, a brief follow-up around day four and a useful resource around day ten. If there is no response, leave the door open.

Proof

Proof points

Established facts and partner benefits to keep every message specific, credible and aligned with the partner page.

Specialist focus. API has specialised exclusively in new property since 2014, with continuous research across more than 10 Australian cities and regions.

Track record. More than 12 years helping hundreds of Australians build wealth through property.

Selectivity. In FY2026, API declined 84% of the properties it assessed. Only 16% reached a client.

Partner confidence. Structured introductions and clear updates from strategy through to settlement, with the client relationship remaining firmly in the partner's hands.

Reform context. From 1 July 2027, negative gearing for residential property will be limited to eligible new builds, giving clients a timely reason to revisit their property strategy.

Collaborative sessions. Co-branded Partner Speaking Sessions are shaped around each partner's client base and hosted by Emma or Chris, online or in person. A limited number of 2026 dates remain.

Direct contacts. Partner conversations are led by Emma Allen (Founder) and Chris Newell (Sales Manager & Principal Consultant).

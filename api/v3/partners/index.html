A partner for the long term

Our relationship with a client rarely ends at settlement. We are there for the second property, the third, the fourth, as strategies evolve and circumstances change.

Many investors begin by simply wanting to get a foot on the property ladder. As their confidence grows, so do their ambitions. Over time, they build sustainable property portfolios, leveraging the success of earlier investments and diversifying across locations and property types to spread risk and access a broader range of market opportunities.

That long-term thinking shapes how we work with our clients, and with the professionals who introduce them. It's built on three values: Quality, Collaboration and Service Excellence.

FAQs

Common partner questions

  • Will API reach out to my client, or do I make the intro?
    Both work. Most partners make an introduction by email, copying in the client and the API team. Either way, the client stays in control of the next step, and you stay informed throughout the process. Partners who refer regularly can also have a dedicated API partner page for client introductions.
  • What kinds of clients are a good referral for API?
    Busy professionals building wealth through property who want strategy before property selection. Often they have the capacity and intent to invest but not the time to research markets, compare opportunities and manage the process themselves. They may be purchasing their first investment property, growing an existing portfolio, diversifying into new markets, or simply have finance in place and need help turning a pre-approval into the right opportunity. If your client wants a strategy-led approach and values specialist support through a new-property purchase, they're likely a good fit for API. There is no cost to your client for our service.
  • What happens to a referral who isn't ready to act yet?
    The client goes onto the Active Investor Path, a named education program with reading, webinars, and a standing invitation to retake the Investor Quiz. You receive a short note explaining why.
  • How is a hosted session shaped for my client base?
    A short planning call helps shape the session around your clients and what you want them to take away. Whether they're considering their first investment or growing an existing portfolio, the content is tailored to their questions and interests. Many sessions explore changes such as the latest tax reforms or the questions clients ask most often. The aim is to add value for your clients and generate more interest in your own service. Availability is limited, so we recommend booking your preferred dates in advance.
  • Is there a cost to me or to my clients?
    The session is offered to partners at no cost. In-person events may involve venue or catering expenses covered by the host partner, discussed during the kick-off call.
  • Is there a referral fee or revenue share?
    Partner arrangements are walked through in the partner onboarding conversation. The specifics depend on the partner type and the regulatory frame you sit in.
  • My firm is not on the partner network yet. Can I still refer or host?
    Yes. Start with the form below. A short conversation first, then we move into the referral or the session from there.

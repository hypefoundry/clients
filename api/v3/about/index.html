Our Philosophy

Property is the vehicle. Strategy is the engine

The outcome of an investment is rarely shaped on the day a contract is signed. It begins much earlier, with the strategy that guides the decision and the research that supports it.

Every opportunity passes through a disciplined research and culling process before it earns consideration. Roughly 84% of the properties we review don't make the cut.

From the beginning, our goal has been simple: replace speculation with structure.

Many clients return for a second, third and even fourth investment. That's the kind of long-term partnership we've always set out to build.

Meet the Founder

Emma Allen, Founder of Active Property Investing

Emma has spent almost two decades in the Australian property industry, including the last twelve years building Active Property Investing.

An active investor herself, Emma has shaped API around a simple belief: successful property investing begins with strategy and is strengthened by disciplined research. That thinking continues to guide the way the team works with every client and every property.

Emma is an award-winning entrepreneur, contributing author to two Amazon best-selling business anthologies, and a podcast co-host.

the reform

What changed on 12 May 2026.

From 7:30pm on 12 May 2026, negative gearing on newly purchased established residential property is restricted to residential property income only. The change was effective immediately. From 1 July 2027, the 50% CGT discount is being replaced. New builds remain fully eligible for negative gearing, including off-the-plan apartments, knock-down rebuilds that add a dwelling, new construction on vacant land, and properties occupied under twelve months before first sale.

  • Now restricted

    Established residential property

    Bought after 7:30pm on 12 May 2026. Rental losses quarantined immediately. No deduction against wages or other income.

  • Still fully eligible

    New property (new build)

    Off-the-plan, KDR with net dwelling increase, vacant land builds, properties occupied under twelve months. Negative gearing unchanged.

Key dates timeline

  • 12 May 2026, 7:30pm

    Negative gearing changes take effect immediately for newly purchased established residential. Grandfathering line drawn at the same moment.

  • Now

    Clients deciding whether to buy established or new are weighing the reform directly.

  • 1 July 2027

    The 50% CGT discount is scheduled to be replaced. Legislation still progressing through parliament.

  • Ongoing

    New builds remain the tax-efficient residential pathway under the new rules.

Common questions from partners.

  • What counts as a "new build" under the reform?
    Eligible new builds include off-the-plan apartments, knock-down rebuilds that increase the dwelling count, residential construction on previously vacant land, and properties occupied less than twelve months before first sale. Regulations are still being finalised.
  • My client already owns established property. What changes for them?
    Nothing, for as long as they hold the asset. Established property owned before 7:30pm on 12 May 2026 (including properties under contract at that time) is grandfathered. Existing arrangements continue under current rules until sale.
  • How is a hosted session shaped for my client base?
    A preliminary call discusses attendees and desired outcomes. Emma builds the session from there. The default frame is the post-budget landscape and what new property does for clients deciding between established and new.
  • Is there a cost to me or to my clients?
    The session is offered to partners at no cost. In-person events may involve venue or catering expenses covered by the host partner, discussed during the kick-off call.
  • What happens to a referral who turns out to be aspiring, not active?
    The client goes onto the Active Investor Path, a named education program with reading, webinars, and an open-door reassessment. Partners receive an explanation of the routing decision.
  • My firm is not on the partner network yet. Can I still refer or host?
    Yes. Start with the form below. A short conversation first, then we move into the referral or the session from there.

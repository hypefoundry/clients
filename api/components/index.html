From 7:30pm on 12 May 2026, negative gearing on newly purchased established residential property is restricted to residential property income only. The change was effective immediately.     New property (new build)

The reform /Update

What changed on 12 May 2026

Share

From 7:30pm on 12 May 2026, negative gearing on newly purchased established residential property is restricted to rental income from that property only.

Effective immediately

The change was effective immediately. Rental losses on established residential property bought after that moment are quarantined and cannot be deducted against wages or other income.

What stays fully eligible

New builds remain fully eligible for negative gearing. That includes off-the-plan apartments, knock-down rebuilds that add a dwelling, new construction on vacant land, and properties occupied under twelve months before first sale.

A second date to hold onto

From 1 July 2027, the 50% CGT discount is being replaced. The two dates together set the frame for every purchase decision from here on.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How does the API process work?
    We start with a short assessment, model a few scenarios, then build a documented strategy you actually understand and agree with.
  • Do I need to be in Sydney to work with you?
    No. We work with partners across Australia. Most sessions happen on video; in-person is available in Sydney.
  • What is included in the partner program?
    Quarterly client briefings, branded session decks, ongoing research access, and a direct line to Emma or Chris when you need a second opinion.
  • How quickly will I get a response?
    We reply to all new partner enquiries within 3 to 5 working days.
  • Do you accept clients in any sector?
    We focus on busy professionals and partner-led referrals. We will tell you up front if your situation is outside our remit.
  • Is there a minimum portfolio size?
    No minimum. We work with first-time investors and 12-property portfolios. The process scales to your situation.
  • What happens after the first conversation?
    You receive a one-page summary of what we discussed and a recommendation for the next step. No pressure to commit.
  • Can I see what others have said?
    Yes. Real client stories sit on the Client Journeys page, including video interviews.

Common questions from partners

  • What counts as a "new build" under the reform?
    Off-the-plan apartments, KDR with a net dwelling increase, vacant land builds, and properties occupied under twelve months from first sale.
  • How are the sessions delivered?
    60-minute webinar or in-person session with Emma or Chris. Limited slots per quarter; audience sized to the room.
  • What if a client is not ready to buy?
    The client goes onto the Active Investor Path, a named education program with reading, webinars, and an open-door reassessment. Partners receive an explanation of the routing decision.
  • Who owns the client relationship?
    You do. API sits behind the scenes — the client-facing brand and communications remain with your firm unless we agree otherwise.

After a referral

Here's what happens after you refer a client

Your client relationship stays yours. We keep you informed at every milestone, from strategy through to property management and well beyond.

  • Strategy

    Every engagement starts with the client's goals and brief, before any property is considered.

  • Research & Review

    Locations and new builds are assessed through a disciplined process, and most are passed on.

  • Contracts

    When a property fits the brief, we guide the client to contracts with clarity on every term.

  • Property Management

    We hand over to property management and stay alongside for the next property, and the one after.

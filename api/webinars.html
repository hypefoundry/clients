01 · HERO
Webinar library. Two audiences: people registering for the next live event, and people coming back for replays. Hero acknowledges both.
Eyebrow

A short tag positioning this as the webinar library.

H1 · Headline

Names what the webinars cover and how often new ones run.

Sub / Value prop

The supporting line. Names the cadence (2-4 webinars per year) and the topics (market briefings, investor education).

02 · NEXT LIVE
Anchor block. The next scheduled webinar with title, date, host, topic, and a registration form. Pre-empts the "is anything happening soon" question above the fold.
Schema: Event schema (startDate, endDate, eventStatus, organizer, location: virtual). Live signpost gives Google + LLMs a discrete upcoming-event datapoint.

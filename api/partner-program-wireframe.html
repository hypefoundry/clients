What changed on 12 May 2026.

3a · Factual brief

Summary · what changed in one paragraph

From 7:30pm on 12 May 2026, negative gearing on newly purchased established residential property is restricted to residential property income only. The change was effective immediately. From 1 July 2027, the 50% CGT discount is being replaced. New builds remain fully eligible for negative gearing, including off-the-plan apartments, knock-down rebuilds that add a dwelling, new construction on vacant land, and properties occupied under twelve months before first sale. Anyone already in the market before the 7:30pm cut-off on 12 May is grandfathered.

3b · Restricted vs eligible

Card A · now restricted

Established residential property.

Bought after 7:30pm on 12 May 2026. Rental losses quarantined immediately. No deduction against wages or other income.

Card B · still fully eligible

New property (new build).

Off-the-plan, KDR with net dwelling increase, vacant land builds, properties occupied under twelve months. Negative gearing unchanged.

3c · Key dates timeline

Timeline panel · 4 rows

12 May 2026, 7:30pm · Negative gearing changes take effect immediately for newly purchased established residential. Grandfathering line drawn at the same moment.

Now · Clients deciding whether to buy established or new are weighing the reform directly.

1 July 2027 · The 50% CGT discount is scheduled to be replaced. Legislation still progressing through parliament.

Ongoing · New builds remain the tax-efficient residential pathway under the new rules.

Sources: Australian Taxation Office, Federal Budget 2026 to 2027, Treasury fact sheets. CGT changes are still being legislated; we will keep this page current as the bill progresses.

Common questions from partners.

FAQ 1

What counts as a "new build" under the reform?

Eligible new builds include off-the-plan apartments, knock-down rebuilds that increase the dwelling count (for example, a duplex replacing a single house), residential construction on previously vacant land, and properties occupied less than twelve months before first sale. We will keep this page current as the regulations are finalised.

FAQ 2

My client already owns established property. What changes for them?

Nothing, for as long as they hold the asset. Established property owned before 7:30pm on 12 May 2026 (including properties under contract at that time) is grandfathered. Existing negative gearing arrangements continue under the current rules until sale.

FAQ 3

How is a hosted session shaped for my client base?

A short kick-off call before the session covers who is in the room, where they are at, and what you want them to walk away with. Emma builds the session from there. The default frame is the post-budget landscape and what new property does for clients deciding between established and new.

FAQ 4

Is there a cost to me or to my clients?

The session is offered to partners at no cost. In-person events sometimes involve venue or catering costs that the host partner covers. We walk through this on the kick-off call.

FAQ 5

What happens to a referral who turns out to be aspiring, not active?

The client goes onto the Active Investor Path, a named education program with reading, webinars, and an open-door reassessment. You get a short note explaining the routing. Right-fit qualification protects your relationship at the point of intake.

FAQ 6

My firm is not on the partner network yet. Can I still refer or host?

Yes. Start with the form above. A short conversation first, then we move into the referral or the session from there.

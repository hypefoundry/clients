The Investor Quiz

There is no single moment that makes you ready to invest in property

Every strategy begins with understanding four practical factors: Finance, Focus, Support and Flexibility. Together they shape the investment brief and the research that follows. The Investor Quiz walks you through each one, giving you a clearer picture of your current position and the most useful next step.

The four factors

Strategy starts here

Before any property is considered, we establish these four inputs. Together they define the investment brief and guide the research that follows.

  • 01

    Finance

    Finance defines the options available. Capital position, ownership structure, borrowing capacity and lender appetite establish the investment brief and the pace at which the strategy can move.

  • 02

    Focus

    Focus gives the strategy direction. Understanding your long-term objectives, competing priorities and current circumstances helps establish a clear investment brief and guides the research that follows.

  • 03

    Support

    Support shapes how the engagement works. Understanding your preferred level of involvement in the research and selection process creates clarity about how we'll work together.

  • 04

    Flexibility

    Flexibility broadens the opportunities the research can explore. A broader brief across locations, property types and markets gives the research more options before narrowing to those that best support the strategy.

After the Investor Quiz

Turning insight into action

The four factors do the work behind the scenes. The result is clear, practical information built around your position.

  • A summary

    A concise summary of what you shared and what it means in context. Useful on its own, whether you continue with API or not.

  • One move

    The single action most likely to strengthen your position and make your next decision easier.

  • A set of options

    Practical ways to continue when you're ready, from educational resources to a strategy conversation. The choice is always yours.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

  • What is the Investor Quiz, exactly?
    A short set of plain questions about where you are and what you want. Your answers help shape what API can usefully say back to you. You receive a summary, an actionable next move, and a clear set of options for what's most useful from here.
  • How long does it take?
    Five minutes. The questions are short. There is no preparation required, and you can complete it from any device.
  • Is the Investor Quiz free?
    Yes. The quiz, the summary, the suggested move, and the options menu are all complimentary. Engaging API for a strategy conversation or formal work is the paid step, and only if you decide it's useful.
  • What happens to my information?
    Your answers stay with API. They're used to shape the summary you receive and inform any conversation that follows. They aren't shared with third parties, and the quiz itself doesn't subscribe you to anything.
  • What if I'm not ready to act yet?
    The options menu always includes a long-arc route. API Property Letters arrive quarterly with market reads and client stories, and the Active Investor Path keeps you close to the strategy while life, finance and timing align. When you're ready to step back in, the context is already there.

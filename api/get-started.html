01 · THE FORM
Single-purpose page. The assessment is the first and only thing on screen. No hero, no preamble, no competing CTAs. Fillout-embedded, 12 questions across 5 pages, mobile-native. Conditional logic routes each completion to Active, Aspiring, or "not the right fit (for now)".
Fillout form embed

A placeholder representing the Fillout assessment form.

12 questions across 5 pages. Page 1 is welcome/context (no fields). Pages 2–5 cover the four factors (Finance, Focus, Support, Flexibility). Final page collects contact details and routes the submission.

Form page 1 · Welcome

Single screen, no fields. Sets expectations and starts the 5-minute clock.

Form page 2 · Finance (3 questions)

Capital, serviceability, buffer.

Form page 3 · Focus (2–3 questions)

Investment goal, methodology curiosity, timeline orientation.

Form page 4 · Support (2 questions)

Decision-maker alignment, household readiness.

Form page 5 · Flexibility (2 questions)

Location openness, strategy openness.

Form page 6 · Contact + outcome

Name, email, phone. Submission triggers the routing logic in §03.

02 · TRUST STRIP
Four short reassurance signals sitting below the form. Catches visitors who scrolled past without filling, and reinforces commitment for visitors mid-form.

Pathway A · Active (16–20)

Names this pathway.

Inline result screen + invitation to book a Strategy Session via Calendly. Auto-tagged as Active in HubSpot.

Pathway B · Aspiring (11–15)

Names this pathway.

Inline result screen + tailored eBook + 90-day nurture sequence kickoff. Re-assessment invite at day 90 and day 180.

Pathway C · Help, not goodbye (4–10)

Names this pathway.

Inline result with a named-archetype message, opt-in partner introductions, and a lighter-touch stay-in-touch list. Not added to the Aspiring nurture.

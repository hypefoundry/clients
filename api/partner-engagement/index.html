The brief

Reconnect with API's existing partner network, strengthen awareness of the current partner offering, and provide a clear pathway for partners to re-engage with the business.

At the same time, create the assets and campaign framework required to support outreach to new referral partners aligned with API's updated positioning and services.

The deliverables include a two-email re-engagement sequence, a campaign landing page, a social outreach kit, and a refreshed Mailchimp template.

What this needs to do

  1. Reconnect with existing partners and bring them up to date with API's current positioning, partner offering, events, and opportunities.
  2. Present a clear and compelling value proposition to prospective partners: who API helps, how the partner program works, and the value provided to both the partner and their clients.
  3. Drive bookings for exclusive partner speaking engagements over the next six months, giving partners a valuable opportunity to engage their clients alongside API.
  4. Capture and segment both audiences within Mailchimp, creating a clean foundation for future partner communications.
  5. Refresh the email template to align with the new website visual direction and provide a consistent experience across future campaigns.

What we'd build

01Re-engagement email sequence

A two-email sequence, designed and built in Mailchimp. Written in your voice and positioned as a meaningful update for existing partners.

The first email re-establishes contact and brings partners up to speed with where API is today. It will be sent to the existing partner list and direct readers to the campaign landing page for further information.

The second email follows seven to ten days later. More value-led in nature, it provides a fresh reason to engage, such as an upcoming speaking event, webinar, or other relevant opportunity for partners.

We'll A/B test subject lines on the first send to gather insights and use those learnings to inform the second email.

02Campaign landing page

The landing page for the email campaign and social outreach will align with the visual direction of the new website.

Its role is to reposition API as the trusted specialist in new property, reinforce its value as a partner resource, and reintroduce the current partner offering. The page will clearly communicate the value available to partners and their clients, how the partner program works, and the opportunities available through the API partner network.

A key objective of the page is to support the booking of exclusive partner speaking engagements over the next six months, delivered either as webinars or in-person events in Sydney. These opportunities will be positioned as a value-add for partners and their clients, with limited availability reflecting API's consulting commitments.

Clear calls to action throughout the page will encourage partners to book a speaking engagement, request a partner introduction, or register for the next webinar.

03Social outreach kit

A self-contained kit for approaching potential new partners on social.

Using the partner profiles supplied by API, I'll develop the outreach framework and supporting assets needed to run the campaign. This will include recommended messaging, follow-up templates, and campaign visuals tailored to the partner audience.

The kit equips you with everything needed to execute the outreach confidently and consistently.

04Mailchimp template refresh

A base email template aligned with the new visual direction of the website. It will include the foundational building blocks required for future campaigns: header, body sections (text, image, button, divider), and footer.

Designed to keep every email on brand without requiring a redesign each time a new campaign is created. Built at the Mailchimp account level so it's available to your full team, not just a single user.

05Setup and handover

Everything required for campaign setup, testing, scheduling, and launch within Mailchimp.

Handover notes will cover how to duplicate and update the Mailchimp template for future sends, how to use the social outreach kit, and how to update the landing page as new events and opportunities arise.

What the quiz already gives us

The Investor Quiz scores four factors, Finance, Focus, Support and Flexibility, and sorts every person who completes it into one of four internal routes.

Quiz route (internal) Summary Ready?
active Clear figure, wants to delegate, open on location Yes
aspiring Capable and keen, one or two pieces not yet in place Close, not yet
self-directed Capable, often finance-ready, wants to run it themselves Not for our service
help Early days, or wants something API doesn't do (e.g. a reno project) No

The Active Investor sequence

Who

Clear on their figure, ready to hand research and shortlisting to a specialist team, open on where the opportunity sits. These are hot leads. The sequence is the safety net for the ones who don't pick up, and a warm-up for the ones who haven't booked.

Example

A qualified active investor requests a call, you try for three days, silence. The sequence keeps the engagement alive and provides several low-friction ways back in, without you spending more calls on it.

Goal

Get them onto the 15-minute strategy call.

Look and Feel

These read like they came from Emma personally. Conversational, professional, warm, direct. A one-to-one email, not a newsletter.

Suggested sequence · 4 to 5 emails · 2 to 3 weeks

Day Email
Day 0 "I've been trying to reach you"
From Emma. Mirrors back what we heard in the quiz (the quiz already writes these lines, e.g. "ready to hand the research and shortlisting to a specialist team"), restates what 15 minutes will do for them, and gives an easy way to reply or rebook.
Day 2-3 "What actually happens on the call"
Removes uncertainty. 15 minutes, no pressure, here's what we cover and what you walk away knowing. Speaks to the one thing that would help them most (the quiz already identifies it, e.g. a broker conversation about borrowing).
Day 5-7 "What we recently found for someone in your position"
A de-identified example: the opportunity, the numbers, why it passed our research. The Research Culling Process doing the selling.
Day 10-12 "Why investors trust us with this"
150+ Google reviews at 5.0, the fact that in FY2026 only 16% of properties we assessed reached a client, and the valet model (we carry the research and the legwork, your life stays intact). Soft, clear CTA.
Day 16-21 "I'll stop chasing you about the call"
The honest last email. Low pressure, high response. Call chasing stops, but they stay on the light nurture below, and the door's open when the timing's right.

Aspiring: "The Active Investor Path"

Who

Capable and genuinely interested, but one or two pieces short of ready, most often finance not yet firmed up, or holding too tight to a single location. The whole idea here is to educate them and be of service while they build toward being ready, so that when they get there, API is the obvious first call.

Example

They read as ready in almost every way, clear on the outcome they want, happy to hand the research and shortlisting to a specialist team, open to where the opportunity sits. The one gap is finance: their figure is not yet firmed up, and the quiz's own read was that the next step is a fresh broker conversation about borrowing and structure. That is a textbook aspiring investor, mentally ready with one practical piece to lock in. They do not need chasing for a call, they need to be kept close and taught until that piece is in place.

Goal

Keep them close and keep being genuinely useful until the missing piece locks in, then move them to a strategy call. No pressure to book today.

Feel

Generous and educational, API teaching rather than selling. Warm, in Emma's voice, useful whether or not they ever buy through API. It should read as a program they joined, "The Active Investor Path".

Tailoring to the weakest factor

In emails one and two, the quiz already knows whether the gap is Finance, Focus, Support or Flexibility, so a finance-capped person gets the borrowing-and-capacity thread first, and a location-rigid person gets the "why the strongest opportunities sit interstate" thread first.

Two emails vary by the weakest factor: the welcome email's resource (email one) and Emma's personal note on "the one area with the most impact" (email two), which is the same factor seen two ways. Everything else in the journey is shared. Out of the four factors we only need three, because a genuine Support gap (wanting to run it themselves) routes to Self-directed or Help, not Aspiring, so it rarely lands here. The resources are the bigger build and most can be adapted from existing content.

Weakest factor Resource angle Likely source
Finance borrowing and capacity, what your figure unlocks rentvesting eBook (finance-led), a broker-conversation explainer
Focus judging on fundamentals over cosmetics Research Culling Process explainer
Flexibility why the strongest opportunities sit interstate a market-openness / interstate piece
Support (fallback only) how much to lean on the team, the valet model existing process / team content

Suggested sequence · ~8 emails over ~90 days · then general mailing list

Day Email
Day 0 "Welcome to the Active Investor Path"
What they've joined, what to expect, no pressure. A first resource matched to their weakest factor.
Day 3 "The one area with the most impact"
A personal note from Emma on their specific gap, and how clients have worked through the same thing.
Day ~10 "Our Research Culling Process"
The Research Culling Process in plain language, or a podcast episode relevant to their gap. Builds trust in the method.
Day ~20 "A real client story"
A market read or client journey relevant to what they came in for (rentvesting, family, SMSF).
Day ~35 "Come to the next webinar"
An invitation to the next live event.
Day ~55 "Someone who started where you are"
A founder-voice email from Emma about a client with a similar profile who got there.
Day ~75 "Anything changed?"
A light check-in, no agenda.
Day ~90 "Retake the quiz whenever you're ready"
A warm re-assessment invitation. Retake and score Active, and they move straight into the Active flow and a call. Still building, and they roll onto the ongoing cadence below.

After the 90 days

General API updates, market notes, events and Property Letters, plus the redacted case-study "property blast" once there has been a progressed deal to feature. It's more education and real proof of what API finds, without handing live opportunities to people who haven't signalled they're ready. Every email includes a small line, "ready to see live opportunities?".

The live blasts themselves stay for people who have signalled readiness (see Enters and exits below). The redacted blast comes online as deals progress and once its template is set.

Enters and exits

Active sequence

  • Enters on completing the quiz on the active route.
  • Exits early when they book a call or reply: the sequence stops immediately.
  • Finishes with no response: they move to the general API updates mailing list.

Aspiring journey

  • Enters on completing the quiz on the aspiring route (enrolled in the Active Investor Path).
  • Exits early when they book a call: they're then handled as an active lead.
  • Finishes the 90 days still building: they roll onto the general API updates mailing list.
  • Retakes the quiz and now scores Active: straight into the Active flow and a call.
  • Clicks "Ready to see live opportunities?": automatically adds the "property ready" tag, which includes them in the property blasts but doesn't remove them from the pathway.

Design review · card 3 (quarterly partner lunches)

Ambient snippets — four density options

All four variants use the same "voices around the table" logic — all-light bubbles floating around the food, no back-and-forth conversation exchange, just overheard fragments. Only the number of bubbles and their positioning changes. Pick the density that feels right.

Variant A · Three snippets, spaced
Third referral this quarter. 1:12 PM
Emma flagged the Bris opportunity. 1:14 PM
Wanting to move by June. 1:16 PM

Three bubbles, generous spacing. Reads as "considered overheard fragments" — restrained, editorial.

Variant B · Three snippets, tighter cluster
Third referral this quarter. 1:12 PM
Emma flagged the Bris opportunity. 1:14 PM
Chris walked us through the reform. 1:16 PM

Same count, tighter cluster in the upper half. Feels more like a live conversation, less like scattered fragments.

Variant C · Four snippets
Third referral this quarter. 1:12 PM
Emma flagged the Bris opportunity. 1:14 PM
Wanting to move by June. 1:16 PM
Chris on the reform Tuesday. 1:18 PM

One more snippet in the mix. Signals more activity, more voices at the table. Risk: starts to crowd the food.

Variant D · Three snippets, no timestamps
Third referral this quarter.
Emma flagged the Bris opportunity.
Wanting to move by June.

A / B but no timestamps. Reads as "quotes" rather than "messages" — less messaging-app, more editorial pull-quote. Loses the "moment in time" cue but gains restraint.

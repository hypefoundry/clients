Design review · card 2 (referrals)

Chat overlay — four treatments

Four ways to overlay the "referral hand-off" chat onto the partner portrait. Each keeps the image identical; only the bubble treatment changes. Pick the one that feels most in-brand.

Variant A · Two-way conversation
Chase family — ready for the reform conversation. Tue 2:14 PM
Got it. Sending them the pre-read now. Just now

Classic messaging pattern — one white bubble (partner out), one dark tiffany bubble (Emma back). Reads as a real hand-off. Most literal.

Variant B · Three snippets, all light
Third referral this quarter. Tue 2:14 PM
Client wants to move by June. Tue 2:15 PM
Emma flagged stock issues in Bris. Tue 2:17 PM

Ambient snippets from the referral pipeline. Less "conversation," more "volume of activity." Signals scale + trust.

Variant C · Tiffany-branded bubbles
Chase family — ready for the reform conversation. Tue 2:14 PM
Got it. Sending them the pre-read now. Just now

Same as A but the light bubble uses tiffany-100 instead of white — reads more brand-owned, less "iOS Messages screenshot."

Variant D · Minimal type-only
Third referral this quarter. 2:14 PM
Client wants to move by June. 2:15 PM
Stock flagged in Bris. 2:17 PM

No bubbles at all. Just white type floating over the darker areas. Most editorial / high-end. Loses the "messaging" metaphor but gains restraint.
